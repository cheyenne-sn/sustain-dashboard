Positive

Disengaged members are responding the most

About a third of attenders were disengaged beforehand. That group nearly tripled their app activity after attending live (+4.9). The programme is reaching and moving the people it's designed for.

Watch

Highly active members dip after attending — that's expected

The drop for highly active members (-8.8 live, -8.6 recording) is most likely a return to their normal level after a peak period, not a negative effect of the webinar.

For discussion

Questions for the team

1

Time is the main barrier — but HC availability makes evenings difficult. Does reducing cadence give us more flexibility?

We've historically run webinars in the evenings but it's hard to get health coach cover consistently. If we move from twice a week to once a week (or once a fortnight), does that free up the ability to lock in a reliable early evening slot? And would fewer, better-attended sessions be more effective than the current volume?

2

The HC rota is changing to include Saturday SWMS cover — could we use this to host a weekend session?

A Saturday slot would serve members who genuinely can't attend on weekdays. If the cover is already being rostered, the additional lift to trial a webinar on that slot may be relatively low. Worth scoping.

3

Registration timing change — early signs are positive

Individual invites now go out one week before each session. Jul 7 Prevent Diabetes: 197 → 441 registrations. Jul 9 Stress: 120 → 321. Jul 14 Sleep: 555 registrations — the highest since launch. Three sessions in a row trending up.

4

Are we reaching the members who most need re-engaging?

Around 28–34% of people engaging with the webinars were disengaged beforehand, and they're showing the strongest response. The next step is to join the attendee data to disengagement flags in Snowflake to confirm how much of our reach is going to the formally identified disengaged cohort.
